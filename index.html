Cardiac Phase & Emotional Memory: BDNF and ADRA2B
Genomics Cognitive Neuroscience Epigenetics

Biology 4231 – Genome Sciences and Genomic Medicine (Spring 2026)

This research proposal investigates how cardiac phase (systole vs. diastole) influences emotional memory consolidation, and whether BDNF Val66Met and ADRA2B deletion variants moderate the "systolic interference" effect. The study integrates ECG timing, genomic stratification, and epigenetic assays (BDNF promoter methylation, histone marks) to predict individual differences in emotional memory.

Key hypotheses: Emotional stimuli may override systolic interference; ADRA2B deletion and BDNF Met carriers show enhanced emotional memory regardless of cardiac phase.

Affiliated with: Pioneer Acadamics & University of Pennsylvania Biology Department
